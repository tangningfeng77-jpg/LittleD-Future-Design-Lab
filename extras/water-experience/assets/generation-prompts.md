# 背景生成记录

工具：内置 imagegen，非 CLI。背景不含手、文字或 UI，手势和所有动态效果由代码叠加。

## lake.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full screen background for interactive environmental water purification exhibit. Create a cinematic photoreal wide 16:9 mountain lake landscape, camera at water level near shore. Bottom 60 percent of image is crystal clear turquoise blue lake with delicate ripples and visible underwater pebbles near bottom edge. Distant green forest shores on left and right converge on layered alpine mountains at center. Horizon at 40 percent down. Soft golden morning sun at upper right, pale clouds blue sky. Beautiful natural documentary photography with blue teal deep green palette. Plenty of calm dark forest at upper left for text overlay. No people, hands, UI, text, logos, buildings or graphics. Output landscape image.

## saline-land.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-screen interactive environmental exhibition background, wide cinematic 16:9. A dry saline agricultural plain in a mountain valley, viewed low from near the ground. Bottom 60 percent is parched cracked brown earth with conspicuous pale white salt crusts and sparse dry tufts, no standing water. Distant alpine mountains and forest at far horizon at 40 percent down, blue sky and a soft golden morning sun upper right. Natural documentary photorealism. A coherent blue mountain / warm ochre earth palette. Leave center foreground unobstructed for an animated technical pump cutaway overlay in HTML. No people, hands, text, equipment, pipes, logos, diagrams, or UI. Beautiful but visibly arid land in need of water.
